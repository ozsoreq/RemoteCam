**App name** (30 max): `Hold That Pose` (14)

**Short description** (80 max):
`Use a second phone as your remote camera. Frame yourself live, then shoot.` (74)

**Category:** Photography · **Tags:** camera, selfie, travel, remote shutter

**Full description** (4000 max):

```
Great photos of yourself — without asking a stranger.

Hold That Pose turns two phones into one remote camera. Prop one phone on a rock, a wall or a
tripod: that's the Camera. Keep the other in your hand: that's the Remote. You see yourself live,
walk into the frame and tap the shutter. The photo lands on both phones.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Live view from up to 30 m away — frame the shot before you take it
• Full-resolution photo saved on the Camera phone, a copy on your Remote
• 3, 5 or 10-second countdown with sounds you can hear from afar
• Best of 3: every tap takes a quick burst and keeps the sharpest shot
• Ultrawide, main and front camera, tap to focus, grid and horizon level
• Mirrored preview, so moving left moves you left
• Works offline — no Wi-Fi, no signal, no account needed
• Tiny download, no ads while you shoot

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-up, no servers, no tracking: photos go straight from one phone to the other
• Safe mode is on by default: each new Remote must be allowed on the Camera phone, which
  always shows a LIVE sign and plays a sound when a Remote connects
• Sessions end automatically when you're done

Made for solo travellers, couples and friends who want the whole scene in the shot.

Both phones need Hold That Pose (Android 8.0 or newer with Google Play services).
Please only photograph people who know and agree.
```

**What's new (first release):** `First release: two-phone remote camera with live view, countdown and safe mode.`
